## 💰 PRICING QUICK REFERENCE

### Your 3 Service Tiers:

**TIER 1: Data Audit & Strategy**
- Price: $1,500–$2,500
- Duration: 2 weeks
- Deliverable: Report + recommendations + 30-min call
- Best for: First-time buyers, exploring fit
- Pitch: "Let's spend 2 weeks diagnosing what's broken"

**TIER 2: Dashboard & System Build** ← Your Bread & Butter
- Price: $4,000–$8,000
- Duration: 4–5 weeks
- Deliverable: Live working dashboard + training + support
- Best for: Companies ready to scale
- Pitch: "4 weeks and we'll build a system your team uses daily"

**TIER 3: Strategic Partnership** ← Path to Recurring Revenue
- Price: $2,000–$5,000/month (or $12K–$16K fixed)
- Duration: 3-month+ retainer
- Deliverable: Ongoing dashboards, strategy, Slack support, optimization
- Best for: Series A+, data-intensive companies
- Pitch: "I become part of your team, not a vendor"

## 📞 SALES PROCESS CHEAT SHEET

```
STEP 1: They see your content (LinkedIn, referral, cold outreach)
        ↓
STEP 2: They click on your portfolio or Calendly link
        ↓
STEP 3: They book a 20-min discovery call
        ↓
STEP 4: You have call, ask qualifying questions, score with BANT
        ↓
STEP 5: You send proposal within 24 hours (if score 14+)
        ↓
STEP 6: They review, ask questions (you answer same day)
        ↓
STEP 7: They say yes, you send 1-page SOW to sign
        ↓
STEP 8: Kickoff → 4-5 weeks → deliver → get testimonial
```

**Conversion Rate:** ~25% of qualified leads close (BANT scoring 14+)
