## 6. TEST & ITERATE
- Try different cold outreach messages, measure response rates
- Try different case studies in proposals, measure close rates
- Test LinkedIn content, measure engagement
- What works? Do more. What doesn't? Stop.

# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Q: Should I update my website immediately?
**A:** Yes, but smartly. Update copy first (doesn't break anything), then Calendly link. Test everything before going live.

### Q: How many cold messages should I send per day?
**A:** 5/day max (Tue–Thu). Any more and you sound spammy. Quality > quantity.

### Q: Should I lower my pricing to get first clients?
**A:** NO. Your first clients pay FULL price. Discount only if they're long-term retainers or you're testing new service.

### Q: What if I get rejected or ghosted?
**A:** Completely normal. 80% of proposals end in "no." 50% of calls are ghosted. This is the game. Move on.

### Q: How do I handle clients who ask "can you do it cheaper?"
**A:** Use objection script from SALES_INFRASTRUCTURE.md. Short version: "You get what you pay for. Cheap dashboards sit unused."

### Q: When should I hire help / bring on a VA?
**A:** After you close 3–4 projects and are consistently booked. Not before. Your next hire should solve a problem you have.
