## PRICING PSYCHOLOGY TIPS

### ✅ DO:
- Lead with value, not price ("Save 10 hours/week" before "$2,000")
- Break big numbers into monthly terms ("$5K project = $1,250/week")
- Show ROI ("This pays for itself in 2 weeks")
- Bundle services (3 dashboards for $4,500 vs. $2,000 each)
- Offer payment plans for larger projects

### ❌ DON'T:
- Undercut competitors to win work (clients assume low quality)
- Quote your lowest price first (always leave room to drop)
- Discount more than 15% (erodes positioning)
- Accept vague scopes ("how much for a dashboard?")
- Work hourly (limits upside, creates dependency)
