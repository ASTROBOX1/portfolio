# 1. SALES PROCESS OVERVIEW

## The 7-Step Sales Funnel (For Tier 2 Prospects — Most Common)

```
STEP 1: AWARENESS
├─ They see problem ("Our dashboards don't work")
├─ Source: LinkedIn post, case study referral, inbound form
└─ Duration: Minutes to weeks

STEP 2: DISCOVERY CALL (15–20 minutes)
├─ Your goal: Confirm they have the problem you solve
├─ Questions to ask:
│  ├─ "What's your main challenge with data right now?"
│  ├─ "Who's involved in this project on your end?"
│  ├─ "When would you want to start?"
│  └─ "What's driving the urgency?"
├─ Their goal: Feel heard, understand if you can help
├─ Red flags: Budget under $2K, no real urgency, decision-maker not on call
└─ Duration: 15–20 minutes (book on Calendly via form)

STEP 3: NEEDS ANALYSIS (If they qualify)
├─ Best format: Async (over email) + 1 follow-up call
├─ Send: "Quick questionnaire" (5 questions about data, timeline, team)
├─ Them: Answer within 24 hours
├─ You: Now you have enough info to scope proposal
└─ Duration: 24–48 hours turnaround

STEP 4: PROPOSAL (Written, <1 page)
├─ Sent via email within 24 hours of call
├─ Format: Problem → Solution → Timeline → Investment → Next Steps
├─ Price type: FIXED (not hourly estimate)
├─ Include: Real numbers ($4K, 4 weeks) not "depends"
├─ Their job: Review internally, ask clarifying questions
└─ Duration: Send same day, expect response in 2–3 days

STEP 5: PROPOSAL REVIEW & CLARIFICATIONS
├─ If yes: Move to agreement
├─ If questions: Answer via email (quick turnaround = good signal)
├─ If no: Ask why ("budget," "timing," "scope"?) — info for future
└─ Duration: 1–2 days for back-and-forth

STEP 6: AGREEMENT & KICKOFF
├─ Send: Simple 1-page Statement of Work (not legal document)
├─ Them: E-sign (takes <5 min)
├─ Schedule: Kickoff call (30 min)
├─ Send: Initial questionnaire ("Tell me about your data sources")
└─ Duration: 1 day (could be same day)

STEP 7: PROJECT EXECUTION
├─ Duration: 4–5 weeks (for Tier 2)
├─ Cadence: Weekly check-ins with them
├─ Deliverables: Staged (dashboard demo week 2, refinements week 3, full launch week 4)
└─ Outcome: Live working system + team trained + documentation

---

TOTAL TIME FROM DISCOVERY CALL TO PROJECT START: 5–10 days
(If prospect is ready to move)
```

# 6. SALES QUALIFICATION FRAMEWORK

## The BANT Scorecard (Do they qualify?)

```
B = BUDGET
├─ Qualified: $2,500+ (minimum Tier 1)
├─ Ideal: $4,000–$8,000 (Tier 2 — your bread & butter)
├─ Red flag: "We want to spend under $500"
└─ What to do if low budget: Offer Tier 1 (audit) instead, or walk

A = AUTHORITY
├─ Decision-maker present: VP, Founder, Head of Dept
├─ Qualified: They can say yes without asking others
├─ Red flag: "I need to check with my CEO / CFO /..."
├─ What to do if not: Include decision-maker in next call

N = NEED
├─ Real problem: Not "nice to have" but "costs us time/money"
├─ Qualified: "This is slowing down X decisions" or "We lose Y to this"
├─ Red flag: "Um, I guess we could use better dashboards?"
├─ What to do if unclear: Ask diagnostic questions (see questions below)

T = TIMELINE
├─ Immediate: "We want to start within 2 weeks" = Qualified
├─ Near-term: "Next quarter" = Qualified but lower priority
├─ Vague: "Sometime this year" = Red flag, probably won't close
├─ No timeline: "Just exploring" = Not qualified for now
└─ What to do: Ask "If this was the right solution, when would you want to start?"

---

QUALIFICATION QUESTIONS TO ASK:

Can you walk me through: [problem they mentioned]?
→ Listening: Is it real pain or theoretical? How badly does it affect them?

Who else on your team is involved in this decision?
→ Listening: If they say "I am" — red flag. Usually needs 2–3 people.

When you say [their problem], what's the impact? Time? Revenue?
→ Listening: Can they quantify it? ($180K means real pain. "inconvenient" = not urgent.)

If we could fix this, when would you want to start?
→ Listening: "Next 2 weeks" = serious. "Sometime next quarter" = exploring.

What's your total budget for this project?
→ Listening: Do they have a number? Is it $1K, $5K, $20K?

Is there anything that would stop this project from moving forward?
→ Listening: Budget, timing, decision-makers, tech constraints?

---

QUALIFY THEM AT END OF CALL:

Score them 1–5 on each criterion (BANT):

B: 5 = Has clear budget, 1 = "Depends on value"
A: 5 = Decision-maker on call, 1 = "I'll check with my team"
N: 5 = Clear pain & cost, 1 = "It would be nice"
T: 5 = "Start in 2 weeks", 1 = "Exploring for later"

TOTAL SCORE:
├─ 18–20 = SEND PROPOSAL immediately (they're ready)
├─ 14–17 = Send proposal with follow-up (they're interested but need pushing)
├─ 10–13 = Long-term lead (come back in 3 months)
└─ <10 = Not ready, pass

---

IF NOT READY NOW, SAY:

"This sounds like a real challenge, and I think I can help. But it feels 
like timing might not be perfect right now. 

How about this: We stay in touch for [timeline]. If [condition], reach out 
and we'll get rolling.

In the meantime, if you think of someone else who might need this, I'd love 
a referral."

→ This keeps the door open without feeling desperate.
```

# 7. COMMON OBJECTION HANDLING

## Objection 1: "We don't have budget"

```
THEIR STATEMENT: "We'd love to do this but budget is tight."

YOUR RESPONSE:

First: Clarify what "tight" means.
"I totally understand. When you say tight budget, are we talking:
├─ A) Under $1K? (different conversation)
├─ B) $1K–$3K? (we can do Phase 1)
├─ C) Just timing? (better in Q2 or Q3?)"

IF THEY SAY UNDER $1K:
"Got it. Honest conversation: $1K doesn't cover the work I do (I'm $2K+ 
minimum). But here's what I'd suggest:

Option 1: Do a smaller diagnostic audit ($2K) that tells you what to fix. 
Then you can decide if full build makes sense.

Option 2: I know some talented junior developers. They're half my rate but 
less strategic. Happy to recommend.

Option 3: Check in when timing is better. No pressure."

IF THEY SAY $1K–$3K:
"Perfect. That's exactly the Tier 1 window. Here's what $2K gets you:
├─ 2-week data audit
├─ Analysis of your key metric
├─ Written recommendations
├─ Strategy call with your team

About 50% of Tier 1 clients move to Tier 2 after seeing value. Just 
exploring at first is totally fine."

IF THEY SAY TIMING:
"When does budget unfreeze? [Listen for date] Great — let's schedule 
a call for [2 weeks before that date] to get planning started."

---

KEY PHRASE TO REMEMBER:
"Budget is never the real problem. Urgency and value perception are. 
If they feel pain, they find budget."
```

## Objection 3: "Let me think about it"

```
THEIR STATEMENT: "This sounds great. Let me think about it and get back to you."

YOUR RESPONSE:

First: Don't just say "OK." Dig deeper.

"I totally understand. Usually when people say 'let me think about it,' 
there's something specific they want to consider. Is it:
├─ A) Budget? (need to check with finance / CEO?)
├─ B) Timing? (need to wait for [something]?)
├─ C) Technical concern? (worried about [X]?)
├─ D) Just want to sit with the idea? (totally fine)"

IF IT'S BUDGET OR TIMING:
"Got it. When would be a good time to follow up? [Date]. Let's schedule 
a quick call for [a week before] to make sure we can move fast."

IF IT'S TECHNICAL CONCERN:
"What's the concern? [Listen]. Here's how I'd approach that: [answer]. 
Does that address it?"

IF IT'S GENUINE "THINK ABOUT IT":
"Perfect. What would help you decide? Another conversation? Client 
reference? Questions I can answer?"

---

KEY RULE:
"'Let me think about it' usually means 'I'm not sold yet.' Your job is 
to find the actual objection and address it."

The worst response: "Sure, I'll check in next week." 
→ They'll avoid you, ghost will happen.

The best response: "Of course. To help you think through it, what's 
the main question in your head right now?"
```

Your developers can build systems, but can they:

Usually the problem isn't building it. It's the planning + handoff.

Here's what I'd suggest: 
├─ I do the strategy and design (2 weeks, $2K)
├─ Your developers build it (they know your tech stack)
├─ I train your team at the end

This is cheaper than me building it, AND your developers own it long-term.

Want to try that?"

OR:

"Total respect for in-house. Here's the question: How long until they 
could start? [if "6 weeks"] 

Would it help to have a working system now while they're building your 
long-term version? I can build fast version in 2 weeks. Then your team 
has something to work from."

---

KEY RESPONSE:
"Building is easy. Strategy + handoff is hard. Let me handle one or both."
